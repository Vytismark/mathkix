COMPANY OVERVIEW
----------------
MathKix is a subscription-based K-5 math learning platform that delivers
personalized, adaptive math practice to elementary-age children. The platform
combines AI-driven level assessment with structured lesson content, giving
parents a transparent view of their child's progress while keeping the child
experience engaging and age-appropriate.

Website: https://mathkix.com
Stage:   Live (product built and deployed; monetization activation in progress)


THE PROBLEM
-----------
Elementary-aged children frequently fall behind in foundational math skills
between school years due to lack of structured, engaging practice at home.
Existing solutions are either too gamified (lacking educational rigor) or too
academic (failing to retain children's attention). Parents lack visibility into
what their child actually knows and where the gaps are.


THE SOLUTION
------------
MathKix offers:

  • Adaptive Learning Engine - AI-powered assessments that identify a child's
    current level and continuously adjust difficulty to keep them in the optimal
    learning zone.

  • Structured Curriculum - Grade-aligned lessons and quizzes covering K-5
    math standards, ensuring what children practice at home reinforces what
    they're learning in school.

  • Parent Dashboard - Real-time progress tracking, quiz history, and level
    data give parents actionable insight without requiring them to sit beside
    their child.

  • 24/7 AI Support - An integrated Claude-powered support system provides
    instant help to parents at any hour, with seamless escalation to human
    agents when needed.

  • Zero Ads - A distraction-free, child-safe environment with no advertising
    of any kind.


TARGET MARKET
-------------
Primary:   Parents of children in grades K-5 (ages 5-11) in the United States
           who are invested in their child's academic performance and willing
           to pay for quality educational tools.

Secondary: Homeschooling families seeking a structured, affordable math
           curriculum supplement.

Market Size:
  - ~33 million K-5 students in the US
  - EdTech for K-12 is a $15B+ market growing at ~16% annually
  - Direct comparable: Prodigy (10M+ users), IXL ($400M+ ARR)


BUSINESS MODEL
--------------
SaaS subscription with three tiers designed to maximize conversion and LTV:

  Plan          Price           Notes
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Monthly       $9.99/month     Low-friction trial entry
  Annual        $79.99/year     ~33% discount; $6.67/mo effective
  Lifetime      $149.99 once    High LTV; ideal for homeschool families

All plans cover one child account with full feature access. Multi-child
family plans represent a near-term upsell opportunity.


COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE
---------------------
  1. AI-First Personalization - Claude API integration enables genuine adaptive
     learning, not just rule-based difficulty switching.

  2. Parent Transparency - Most competitors focus on the child; MathKix treats
     the parent as an equal stakeholder with full visibility.

  3. Clean, Focused UX - Purpose-built for K-5 math only. No bloat, no ads,
     no distractions - a deliberate contrast to broad edtech platforms.

  4. Lifetime Tier - Creates a loyal, vocal early adopter base and generates
     upfront cash flow during the growth phase.

  5. Low Overhead - Fully serverless architecture (Vercel + Supabase) means
     infrastructure costs scale with revenue, not ahead of it.


TECHNOLOGY
----------
The platform is fully built and deployed on a modern, scalable stack:

  • Frontend / Backend:  Next.js 16 (App Router), TypeScript
  • Database & Auth:     Supabase (PostgreSQL)
  • Payments:            Stripe (subscription billing)
  • AI:                  Anthropic Claude API (assessment + support)
  • Styling:             Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/ui
  • Hosting:             Vercel (global CDN, serverless)

The architecture is intentionally lean - no dedicated servers to manage,
automatic scaling, and a clean TypeScript codebase that is maintainable by
a small team.
